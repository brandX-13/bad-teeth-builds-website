Bad Teeth Builds App Support

Need help using the Bad Teeth Builds motorcycle maintenance and diagnostics reference app? We're here to help with app-related questions, technical issues and feedback.

Contact support

Email brandxtattoo@hotmail.com. Please include what happened, whether you're using iPhone or Android, and the app version if you know it. Do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

EMAIL APP SUPPORT

Quick help

Garage or settings: Use the Garage section to manage saved motorcycles and Settings to adjust your preferences.

Diagnostics: Search diagnostic codes directly; you do not need to select a motorcycle first. Diagnostic information is a reference, not proof that a specific component has failed.

Videos won't open: Check your internet connection. The app's built-in reference information and the Customer app's bundled thumbnail images are available offline, but opening YouTube videos requires network access.

Something looks incorrect? Send us the code or reference section and describe the issue so we can review it.

Privacy and local data

Saved Garage information and preferences are held in the app's local storage. You can remove individual saved motorcycles in Garage. To learn more about local data, deletion and external services, read our privacy policy.

Safety reminder

Bad Teeth Builds provides educational motorcycle reference information. Confirm procedures and specifications for your installed equipment, follow safety warnings, and consult qualified service assistance when needed. Never consult the app while operating a motorcycle.
